An old money fragrance is noticed only by the person standing close to you. It never arrives in the room before you do.

These two cover the whole week — a clean Italian cologne for the day and a warm, woody one for the evening.

01Acqua di Parma Colonia Pura Eau de Cologne

Italian freshness in a century-old tradition

Colonia Pura opens with bergamot, jasmine sambac and petitgrain, with narcissus at the heart and a soft base of musk, patchouli and cedarwood. It is fresh and clean from morning to evening, and never loud.

The story you will enjoy telling: Acqua di Parma was founded in Parma, Italy, in 1916. Its yellow box is recognised by anyone who knows fragrance — and Colonia Pura joined its famous Colonia family in 2017.

Note: the Amazon listing may show Acqua di Parma's earlier grey-label packaging. The fragrance inside is the same Colonia Pura.

02Givenchy Gentleman Boisée Eau de Parfum

The evening one

Gentleman Boisée opens with black pepper, settles into iris and cocoa, and finishes on sandalwood and patchouli. Warm and woody rather than sweet, it belongs at dinner rather than the desk.

What makes it special: Givenchy makes it in France, in its own ateliers, and one of its base notes is patchouli from Indonesia.
